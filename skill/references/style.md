# Observed style and practical implementation

## Cream/orange character treatment — supplied coaching video

Observed in FjPuSavm3NY. Warm ivory field with faint regular dots, broad orange paper
stains and very subtle grain. Black heavy rounded sans text is usually left aligned near
the upper left; white words sit on tight orange rectangles for narrative stress. Expenses
and corrections use pink/red. White cards have small corner radii, soft contact shadows,
little labels and one dominant amount. Characters are compact rounded people and money
objects with gradients, dark outline, face, edge highlight, rim and cast shadow.

Use this approximate production palette, selected from the local frames and intentionally
adapted for the original demo: paper #f1ebe1, ink #191714, orange #fb790b, deep orange
#b94705, white #fffdf8, correction #e94f70. Green #33735c is an original demo addition for
the final remainder; it is not claimed as the coaching video's dominant accent.

Composition is sparse within a richly textured field. The eye follows a clear relationship:
speaker/text at upper left, one protagonist or amount, then relevant props below or beside.
Camera/reframe accents are short; long sections hold readable composition while individual
words, chips or objects animate. Do not impose constant dramatic camera movement on this
variant merely because another motion skill recommends it.

  full-line typewriter. f32–42 (~0.533–0.700 s): the orange coin enters from the right and

  122.53–122.85 s: a white speech card expands/settles with text resolving inside it.
- 380.08–380.50 s: one labelled block falls onto a lower pair, then another arrives around
  381.18–381.57 s. The action makes the limit/capacity metaphor visible.
- 651.08–651.35 s: a balance appears while the spoken-text emphasis builds. Around
  652.60–652.90 s a supporting note appears below. Do not make all three happen together.
- 150.82–151.60 s: ledger reframes closer and the total rapidly counts to ₹90,000 with
  blurred intermediate digits, then tiny confetti appears. 153.07–153.37 s: a lateral
  blur transition carries the ledger out and people in. 154.30–154.65 s: the same ledger
  enters again, smaller beside the person. Around 157.22–157.65 s a speech card unfolds.
- 176–177.4 s: an existing remainder bar holds while the pink cost character approaches.
  177.42–178.0 s: a new expense is subtracted; a pink block drops below the bar and the
  amount rolls down to ₹1,166. The character exits around 178.5–178.8 s. This is the most

These intervals bound the visible changes; easing functions and blur shutter settings were
not recovered. For a fresh build, sensible approximations are 0.15–0.35 s word reveals,
0.25–0.55 s prop entries with small overshoot, and 0.10–0.25 s reframe accents. Judge their
result rather than asserting those are the source's exact numerical settings.

the unexplained remainder.

## Shared story grammar, not universal surface rules

All three local overviews show unit prices, counts, costs, remainder, repeated units and
physical explanatory devices. They do not establish that every upload must be orange,
cartoon-led, or 3D. Match the selected variant while keeping the unit-economics story clear.
Audio transcription of the coaching intro suggests a direct second-person hook, a numerical
gap, then a household story. Audio style notes from Vertex are marked as model reports,
not claims that the local agent listened to playback.
